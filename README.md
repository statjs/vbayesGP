# R Package for Variational Gaussian Approximation of Bayesian Semiparametric Models with Gaussian Process Priors

- The 2023 research grant from the Occupational Safety and Health Research Institute(OSHRI), Korea.
- Basic Science Research Program through the National Research Foundation of Korea(NRF) funded by the Korea government(MSIT) (RS-2023-00209229).
- National Research Foundation of Korea(NRF) grant funded by the Korea government(MSIT) (no. 2021R1A2C1014409).

# References:
- Seongil Jo, Shinhee Ye, Georg Hahn, and Woojoo Lee (2026). Accelerated Bayesian Kernel Machine Regression: A Gaussian Variational Approximation with the Horseshoe Prior. 36:79.

# Installation:
- From the files in this repository (the source tarball works on every platform; the binaries need no compiler):

    ### macOS (arm64), R 4.5
    install.packages("https://github.com/statjs/vbayesGP/raw/main/vbayesGP_1.4.1.tgz", repos = NULL)
    ### Windows
    install.packages("https://github.com/statjs/vbayesGP/raw/main/vbayesGP_1.4.1.zip", repos = NULL)
